# bcdec

HeavenBench uses the `bcdec` BC4 block decoder from:

- Project: https://github.com/iOrange/bcdec
- Revision: `93628fe5627102fe5187b7eeb99122dec6612c36`
- License: MIT License or The Unlicense

The decoder is called through HeavenBench's bounded, AssetStream-compatible
DDS channel facade. The upstream source is not modified.
